How to memorialize a Facebook account. Facebook allows a designated "legacy contact" to manage a memorialized account. To check if a legacy contact is already set: Settings → Memorialization Settings. To request memorialization from outside the account, submit a Special Request at facebook.com/help/contact/305593649477238 with proof of death (obituary or death certificate). The profile will get a "Remembering" label and the legacy contact can pin posts and manage the profile.

How to Request Account Deletion

Each platform has its own process. In most cases you will need a death certificate and proof of relationship.

  • Facebook/Instagram: Use the Special Request form or memorialization request linked above.
  • Twitter/X: Contact support via the Help Center or submit a deactivation request with proof of death.
  • LinkedIn: Use the "Deceased Member" form at linkedin.com/help/linkedin/ask/ts-rdmlp.
  • Google (YouTube, etc.): Use Google's Inactive Account Manager or submit a request through their deceased user support page.
