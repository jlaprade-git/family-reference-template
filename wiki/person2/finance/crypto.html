Crypto & Digital Assets

Wallets, exchanges, seed phrases, and how to access them.

Crypto wallets are controlled by whoever has the seed phrase / recovery words. If these are lost, the funds are gone forever. Seed phrases should be stored physically (written down, never digitally) in the safe.

How to Access Self-Custody Funds

  1. Find the seed phrase (12 or 24 words) in the safe
  2. Download the wallet software (or use the hardware wallet if applicable)
  3. Choose "Restore / Import wallet" and enter the seed phrase
  4. The wallet and all funds will appear
  5. Never enter a seed phrase on a website or share it with anyone

Important Notes

  • Do not rush. Crypto doesn't expire. Take your time and find a trusted person to help if needed.
  • Beware of scams. No legitimate service will ever ask for your seed phrase. If someone offers to "help recover" your crypto and asks for the seed phrase, it's a scam.
  • If selling, use a reputable exchange (Coinbase, Kraken, etc.) and transfer the funds to a bank account.
