Important Tax Dates
  • January 31 -- W-2s and 1099s should arrive by this date.
  • April 15 -- Federal and most state tax filing deadline.
  • June 15 / September 15 -- Quarterly estimated tax payments (if applicable).
  • October 15 -- Extended filing deadline (if extension was filed).

If a death occurs during tax year, a final return still needs to be filed for that year. The CPA or estate attorney can help with this.
