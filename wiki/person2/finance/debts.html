Personal Debts / IOUs

[FILL IN: Any informal debts, loans from family/friends, money owed to or by you.]

What happens to debts? Most debts do not automatically pass to a surviving spouse unless they are co-signed. The mortgage stays with the house -- if you keep the house, you keep the mortgage. Federal student loans are discharged upon death. Private student loans depend on the lender. Credit card debt in only one person's name is paid from the estate, not the surviving spouse's pocket. Consult the estate attorney for specifics.
