Death Certificates

You'll need multiple certified copies of the death certificate (at least 10-15). The funeral home usually helps you order these. You'll need them for:

  • Each bank / financial institution
  • Life insurance claims
  • Social Security
  • Vehicle title transfers
  • Property / mortgage company
  • Employer
